Two brothers, one booth.

Maestro Productions is Kyle Hope and Bryan Hope. We grew up around records, rooms, and the kind of nights people still talk about. We bring that standard to your date: prepared, calm, and present.

Bryan is based in Indianapolis. Together we plan the night, run the booth, and stay with the room until the last song. You get both of us in the planning, and someone who is actually listening on the night.

What we bring

  • Music that fits the people in the room
  • A timeline you can hand to a planner
  • MC support when you want it, and quiet when you don't
  • Clear setup, teardown, and day-of communication

How a date with us goes.

Three steps from first message to last song.

  1. 1

    The conversation

    You tell us about the room, the people, and the songs you can't do without. We tell you what we need to make the night feel easy.

  2. 2

    The plan

    A simple timeline: ceremony, dinner, first dances, open floor. Must-plays and do-not-plays. No surprises for you or your coordinator.

  3. 3

    The night

    We arrive early, stay present, and adjust when the room asks for it. The goal isn't a loud set. It's a night that feels like yours.
